Vastu Rules for Bedroom:
- The bed should ideally be placed in the South or West direction of the room.
- Avoid placing a mirror directly facing the bed.
- Heavy furniture like wardrobes should be placed in the South, West, or South-West corner.
- The bedroom door should ideally open at 90 degrees and face East or North.
- Avoid sleeping with your head pointing North; South or East is preferred.
- Do not place a TV in the bedroom; if unavoidable, keep it in the South-East corner.
- Keep the bedroom clutter-free to allow positive energy flow.

Vastu Rules for Living Room:
- The TV should be installed in the South-East corner.
- Sofas and heavy furniture should be placed in the West or South direction.
- The main entrance to the living room should ideally face East or North.
- Avoid placing furniture in the center of the room; keep it free for energy flow.
- Light colors like white, yellow, or green are recommended for living room walls.
- Plants can be placed in the East or North corner of the living room.
- Electronics and electrical appliances should be in the South-East corner.

Vastu Rules for Kitchen:
- The kitchen should ideally be located in the South-East corner of the home.
- The cooking stove should be placed in the South-East direction.
- The person cooking should face East while cooking.
- The refrigerator should be placed in the South-West or North-West corner, not in the North-East.
- Sink and water-related items should be in the North-East corner.
- Avoid placing the kitchen directly below or above a toilet or prayer room.

Vastu Rules for Bathroom:
- The bathroom should ideally be in the North-West or West direction of the home.
- Avoid placing the bathroom in the North-East or South-West corner.
- The toilet seat should face North or South, never East or West.
- The shower or tap should be on the North or East wall.
- Keep the bathroom door closed as much as possible to prevent negative energy flow.

Vastu Rules for Study Room:
- The study room should ideally be in the West, North, or East direction.
- The student should face East or North while studying for better concentration.
- The bookshelf should be placed on the East, North, or North-East wall.
- Avoid placing the study table in front of a wall with no window.
- Keep the study room well-lit with natural light from the East.
